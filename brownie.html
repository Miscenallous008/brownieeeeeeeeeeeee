Dear Angel,

Happy birthday to one of those people who can make an ordinary moment feel fun just by being there. I hope you know how special your energy is — the jokes, the random silliness, the playful 😝 moments and the way you can make people smile without even trying.

You have such a good nature. You care, you help, you support people and you show up in ways that matter. Those things might feel small to you, but they can mean a whole lot to someone else. Never think you have to be anything other than the person you already are to be appreciated.

For this new year of your life, I hope you get gentle mornings, loud laughs, happy surprises, people who choose you back, dreams that come closer and so many reasons to feel proud of yourself. I hope you keep your beautiful heart while learning to save some kindness for yourself, too.

Stay funny. Stay kind. Stay a little chaotic. Keep being your cheerful, playful self — the world needs more people who bring this kind of light. And whenever you forget how wonderful you are, come back here and borrow a reminder. 🤍

Happy birthday, you lovely little human. ♡
